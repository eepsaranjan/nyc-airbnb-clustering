From raw listings to clean features

Before any clustering happens, the data has to earn its place in the model.

01

Clean

Duplicates removed, missing values in name, host name and review fields resolved, and zero-price or extreme outlier listings capped using percentile bounds.

02

Explore

Around fifteen charts covering price distribution, borough and room-type mix, geographic spread, review activity and host concentration, plus a word cloud of listing titles.

03

Engineer

Categorical fields one-hot encoded; three ratio features built (price per review, reviews per listing, availability ratio); listing titles cleaned and TF-IDF vectorized for text-mining insight.

04

Reduce

Numeric features standardized and compressed to two components with PCA, purely for visualizing how the clusters separate.

Three ways to draw the boundaries

Each algorithm sees the same listings differently — the comparison is what makes the final choice defensible.

K-Means chosen

Tuned with the elbow method and silhouette score. Scales to the full dataset, lands on five roughly-sized, easy-to-explain segments, and held up on a train/test stability check.

Agglomerative Hierarchical runner-up

Tuned by linkage type and visualized with a dendrogram. Closely corroborated the K-Means structure, but needed sampling to run on data this size.

DBSCAN diagnostic

Grid-searched over eps and min_samples. Doesn't force every listing into a group — its noise points usefully flag unusually sparse or isolated listings worth a closer look.

The five segments

What K-Means (k=5) found when the listings were profiled against the overall average.

1

The Brooklyn regulars

Popular, frequently-booked entire homes concentrated in Brooklyn.

2

The professional hosts

Higher-priced, low-turnover entire-home listings in Manhattan, run by hosts managing many listings each.

3

The Staten Island quiet corner

A small niche group of private rooms with high availability but very few reviews.

4

The Brooklyn budget rooms

Lower-cost private rooms with modest, steady review activity.

5

The Queens small hosts

Mid-priced private rooms run by hosts managing a handful of listings.
